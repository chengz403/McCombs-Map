## Key areas (GSB wing)

- **Ford Career Center 4.114** – curved front desk area facing the escalators; 4.114 offices (AA–FG, DA–DH, EA–EP, FA–FE) behind it.
- **NRG Productivity Center 4.112A** – large open room on the diagonal west edge, with offices 4.112AE–4.112AR. Always label it "NRG Productivity Center" (the in-building sign still says Reliant; ignore that).
- **AIM Reading Room 4.112B**, **MPA Program Office 4.112CA**, offices 4.112CB–4.112CP along the **balcony** on the diagonal east edge.
- **Recruiting Operations / Ford Career Center Recruitment Services** – interview rooms 4.126A–L, 4.128A–J, 4.100A–H.
- **Core:** elevators, escalators, restrooms, 4.102–4.124, stairs at the NW and SE corners.
- **CBA connections:** two "CBA" passages on the south side lead into the CBA wing.

## Key areas (CBA wing, from the photo only)

- Atrium in the middle, ringed by classrooms 4.304–4.350, TA Room 4.304A, Storage 4.302/4.334, MPA Lounge 4.352.
- East block: Management 4.202, offices 4.204–4.248, conference room.
- Black triangular skylight structure south of the CBA escalators.

## Project direction

- **Main goal:** wayfinding – get someone from where they are to a classroom.
- **Enterable rooms:** some rooms should be walkable inside, not just destinations. First candidates: **NRG Productivity Center 4.112A** and **Ford Career Center 4.114**. Leave real doorway openings into these when building walls.
- **Out of scope for now:** the Fourth Floor Mezz (4M.2xx). Don't model it yet.
